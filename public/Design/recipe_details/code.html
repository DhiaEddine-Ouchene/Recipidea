Rustic Heirloom Tomato Pizza

A crispy, naturally leavened crust topped with the season's best heirloom tomatoes, fresh mozzarella, and a drizzle of garlic-infused olive oil.

Instructions

1

Preheat Oven

Place a pizza stone or inverted baking sheet in the oven and preheat to 500°F (260°C) for at least 30 minutes. This ensures a perfectly crispy crust.

2

Prepare the Base

In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oil and minced garlic. Gently stretch the room-temperature dough into a 12-inch circle on a piece of parchment paper. Brush the dough generously with the garlic oil.

3

Assemble Pizza

Distribute the torn mozzarella evenly over the base. Arrange the heirloom tomato slices on top, overlapping slightly if necessary. Sprinkle with a pinch of salt.

4

Bake and Garnish

Carefully transfer the pizza (on the parchment paper) onto the preheated stone. Bake for 10-12 minutes until the crust is deeply browned and cheese is bubbly. Remove from oven, top immediately with fresh basil, flaky sea salt, and black pepper.
